WELCOME TO THE ULTIMATE GAME OF...

Tic-Tac-Toe

THE RULES ✨

Rule 1

Two-player game, so grab a partner and look into thier eyes and say "are you ready to lose!"

Rule 2

You alternate placing your mark in an empty square, aiming to get three in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally).

Rule 3

Player to do so wins; if all nine squares fill up without this, the game is a draw! Just hit "Rematch" to start again.
